Statement of claims discipline. This document makes no performance claims about any device. EnergyWave has no prototype, no measured output and no validated energy-conversion mechanism, and nothing here should be read as an assertion that such a mechanism exists or has been demonstrated. What it asserts is narrower: (1) portable energy density is the binding constraint on ambulatory robotic platforms, and the constraint is worsening; (2) incremental electrochemical improvement will not close the gap on the timeline on which autonomy is arriving; (3) a small, disciplined research program with public retirement criteria is a low-cost, high-information way to test whether any non-chemical approach merits further national attention — and to close the question publicly if it does not. The field has a long history of unfalsifiable claims; this program intends to be legible as the opposite.
1 · The bottleneck

Progress in information technology has compounded for five decades. Progress in portable energy density has not. Transistor counts have risen by roughly seven orders of magnitude since 1971; practical specific energy in portable storage has risen by less than one. Every autonomous physical system inherits that divergence: the intelligence is arriving, the power to move it is not.

The peer-reviewed literature on robot energetics puts humanoids at 150–400 W at rest against 60–80 W for a human, and 5–10× less efficient than humans in locomotion [12]. The gap between specification and field performance is itself a recognized problem: published runtimes are derived in controlled environments against favourable duty cycles. A fair summary is that today’s humanoids carry a few kilowatt-hours and run for hours, not days, at a few hundred watts average; the industry’s current answer to endurance is logistics — swap packs, dock, run in shifts — not energy.

1.2 Why actuation is the hard case. The load is not computation; it is mechanical work. Degrees of freedom added for dexterity are potentially major, task-dependent loads: holding a position under gravity draws power unless the actuator or transmission cancels or locks the load, and walking draws substantially more. Secondary coverage puts actuators at a large share of a production humanoid’s bill of materials — a cost share, not a power share [TBD] — a primary source for v2.0’s “56 % of BOM; 28 body plus 50 hand actuators”, or the figures are omitted. Moore’s Law made the thinking cheap. It did nothing for the lifting.

1.3 Why chemistry will not close it. Lithium-ion is approaching its material ceiling: commercial cells were about 220 Wh/kg in 2020 against a program goal of 500 Wh/kg [13]; the best production cells reach 260–271 Wh/kg and packs 120–200 Wh/kg once housing, cooling and electronics are added [14]. Industry planning assumptions put improvement at roughly 4 % per year [6]; at that rate a doubling takes about eighteen years. Platform manufacturers are not waiting — they are engineering around it with inductive charging, swappable packs and high-nickel chemistries accepted for their density at a cost and thermal penalty. These are competent responses to a hard constraint, and an admission of it.

2 · Why this is a defense problem

The commercial case tolerates a charging dock; the defense case often does not. The dismounted power burden is a decades-old, unsolved logistics problem: a dismounted soldier in Afghanistan carried roughly 9.7 pounds of batteries, unable to recharge away from a vehicle or forward operating base, with battery weight projected to exceed 14 pounds for a 72-hour mission as every soldier is brought into the network [8]. Allied assessments place the burden as high as 25 % of a soldier’s carried load on some mission profiles [9]. The Army has stated the trend plainly: across all six modernization priorities there has been a dramatic increase in demand for power as a result of energy-hungry new capabilities [10]. Add an ambulatory robotic platform to that formation and, at current energy density, the unit either tethers to a resupply and recharge cycle or does not deploy. Endurance becomes a doctrinal constraint, not an engineering footnote.

The strategic question follows: a five-fold improvement in portable energy density at the point of use changes force structure; a 4 %-per-year improvement does not. That gap is worth a small, well-governed research investment even at low probability of success, because the payoff is asymmetric and the cost of being second is high.

3 · What is proposed

A research program, not a product program. 3.1 The protocol. Every candidate mechanism proceeds through three gates, in order, with none skipped. Characterize: establish precisely what the claimed effect is, under what conditions, with what instrumentation, and what the null hypothesis predicts; reproduce the original measurement setup where records permit. Conservation-test: subject the effect to closed-system energy accounting with every input path measured — ambient thermal, RF, magnetic and mechanical included; if the output is accounted for by a conventional input, the mechanism is reclassified as a transducer and evaluated on those honest terms. Extract or retire publicly: an effect that survives proceeds to engineering characterization; one that does not is published as a negative result under the company’s own name. Negative results in this field are almost never published, which is why the same claims recirculate for decades and why serious institutions will not engage. A retired claim is a deliverable.

3.2 The candidate threads. Three threads run under the one protocol: the founder’s solid-state module in the Sweet lineage (thread A); an independent, pre-registered replication of a peer-reviewed report of unexplained output from a metal–insulator–metal diode under a Casimir cavity — 1.4 pW maximum inferred load power, one laboratory, not independently replicated in the reviewed evidence, with a published critique predicting the opposite sign (thread B); and the measurement platform itself, whose error budget is published as a methods paper before any result is claimed (thread C). The xTech Search 10 entry proposes thread B as an independent evaluation capability for Army sustainment, and that proposal stands as submitted. The founder’s solid-state module (thread A) is the lead research path — the founder’s decision of 15 October 2026 (D2); a selected lead path is not measured performance or an operational schedule, and threads B and C remain distinct workstreams under the same protocol.

3.3 What is not claimed. The physics literature bearing on vacuum-energy extraction is not favourable: peer-reviewed reviews state that continuous cyclic extraction of this kind would violate the second law of thermodynamics, and that the kilowatt-scale extraction figures in that literature are unbuilt theoretical estimates rather than measured results. Moddel et al. report small electrical observations — picowatt-scale inferred load power — whose interpretation is disputed and whose independent net-cycle validation is not established in the reviewed evidence; those observations are distinct from any proof of a vacuum source. The company’s theoretical physicist and its senior engineering advisor have been consistent on this point. Its theoretical physicist has assessed at least one historically prominent solid-state approach as non-viable as described; its senior engineering advisor, whose career runs from MIT research through commercialization of cryogenic systems for MRI and superconducting electronics, has held that the second law is not incomplete. The Department is not asked to accept a claim. It is asked whether competent instrumentation and honest accounting applied to specific historical claims, with publication in either direction, is worth doing.

4 · Control and safety architecture

If any high-density portable energy source becomes practical, its control architecture is the enabling condition for release, and the program designs to that assumption from day one. Platform binding: the module is scoped to ambulatory robotic platforms and cryptographically bound to a specific host; it authenticates to the platform controller and delivers no output to an unrecognized host — a keyed consumable. Application scoping in hardware: power ceiling, ramp rate and duty envelope are fixed to the actuation and compute envelope of an ambulatory platform; repurposing as a bulk supply would require defeating hardware limits, a detectable and destructive act. Tamper-responsive inertion: envelope breach, unauthorized disassembly, out-of-spec thermal or electrical conditions or authentication failure trigger an irreversible transition to an inert state; no service mode, no recovery path, no field-repairable configuration. Custody and revocation: units are individually identified, attested and remotely revocable; a recovered or diverted unit is a dead unit.

Negative scope, intended as binding conditions of any government relationship: no directed-energy application; no propulsion application; no munitions or ordnance integration; no general-purpose or grid-scale configuration; no export or foreign transfer absent explicit U.S. Government authorization. The company would welcome these being written into an agreement. Disclosure posture: controlled rather than open; the time to establish government visibility is before results exist, not after. The Compartmentalization and Anti-Tamper Posture memo (v0.1, Sept 2026) sets out the bins, the kill, the platform binding and the questions for counsel.

7 · The ask

A technical review meeting to discuss: (1) whether the ambulatory-platform power constraint in §§1–2 is recognized as a capability gap within relevant program offices; (2) whether a conservation-tested characterization program with public retirement criteria is of interest as a low-cost, high-information investment; (3) whether the control architecture in §4 should be developed under Government specification, independent of the energy research outcome; (4) what framework would govern disclosure, custody and scope restriction should Phase 1 produce a positive result. The company is not asking for validation of a claim. It is asking whether this question is worth answering carefully, by people willing to publish a negative.
