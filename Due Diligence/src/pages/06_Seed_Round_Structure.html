The round the bridge is built to reach. Capital follows evidence.

Objective #3 of the capital-readiness bridge is “define measurable go / no-go criteria and prepare the next round.” This page is the structure of that round as the program documents already describe it. The amount, the pre-money and the instrument are the founder’s decisions with counsel and are not stated here.
