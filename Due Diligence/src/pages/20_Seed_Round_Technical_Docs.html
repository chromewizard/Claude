Scope statement — read before any document in this folder
No measured EnergyWave hardware performance evidence has been reviewed for this package. Every number in an EnergyWave-authored document is labelled by origin — assumption · calculation · proposed target · company measurement · attributed published result — and no EnergyWave document carries a company measurement yet. Figures cited from published research are attributed to their authors and are not EnergyWave results. Every claim the company examines must answer two questions: what is the net energy source, and where is the colder sink? The reviewers’ refinement is adopted here: a prepared, stored or driven state is itself an answer to the first question and is counted as an input; a colder sink is the test for a heat-engine claim, and the release of a stored potential is accounted as storage, never as generation.

Energy accounting — the standard every thread is held to
Eall inputs = Euseful load + Eother leaving + ΔUdevice, where U includes everything stored in capacitors, batteries, magnetic and strain states and internal thermal reservoirs. Preparation, drive, control, cooling and reset energy are counted once each; a reset already inside a full-cycle measurement is not counted twice. DC input is ∫v(t)i(t)dt, never average V × average I; probe deskew in a high-Q circuit can turn a true +50 mW dissipation into a false −75 mW “generation” reading at 1 MHz. A null result bounds the tested configuration at its demonstrated sensitivity — it does not identify an origin. A voltage recovery, a resonant amplification ratio, a short-circuit current trace or a single discharge is an observation, not a useful-load energy measurement.

The reported four-hour trace is short-circuit current, not metered useful-load energy. Vacuum excitation is the authors’ proposed interpretation. Maclay 2024 argues the cavity model predicts the wrong current direction and the wrong cavity-size behaviour — a critique of mechanism, not a replication. No independent replication establishing vacuum origin and a complete sustained net-output system was located in the reviewers’ bounded search.

A finite discharge with a paid reset: the cycle is non-positive, not a repeating net-power engine. Carried as a one-shot store in Prospectus §6.5.
