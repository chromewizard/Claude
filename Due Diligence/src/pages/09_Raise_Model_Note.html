Dollars tied to milestones, not an open-ended ask. A reading note for the workbook.

The workbook in this folder is a 36-month, driver-based cash-need plan for EnergyWave, Inc. only. It computes a raise size from the plan — headcount, laboratory, equipment, G&A and reserve — and tranches it by the three research gates. It contains no device performance claims and no technical specifications.
