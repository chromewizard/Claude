Seed Round — the round the bridge is built to reach. Capital follows evidence.

EnergyWave is an early-stage research venture building the power supply for humanoid robots — and first finding out, to a published standard, whether any non-chemical approach can beat the lithium cell a humanoid already carries. The seed round funds that program to its first decision, and pays for the next decision only if the first says go.

  • The problem. Compute per dollar grew ~10⁶× since 1990; portable energy density ~3–4×. Whether a humanoid running human-grade sensing and dense actuation at once exceeds what a lithium pack delivers is the question the power-budget specification quantifies for a defined platform; the industry’s answer to endurance today is swap packs and docks. Directional; attributed figures.
  • The work. Three threads — the founder’s solid-state module (A, the lead path — founder, 15 Oct 2026), an independent replication of Moddel et al. 2021 (B), the artifact-exclusion measurement platform (C) — under one gate protocol. Mainstream prior: no. Published either way.
  • The discipline. Pre-registered protocols; replication contracted before each gate; a planned advisory board with a declared critic and a veto over performance statements; no number before Gate-3 third-party validation.
