How to read this package. What is verified, what is open, and in what order.

EnergyWave is an early-stage research venture. This package is built so that a careful reader can tell, on every page, which statements rest on documents, which rest on a dated communication, and which are decisions not yet taken. Nothing here claims that a device exists, works, or will work.

The one rule that governs every page
Two questions. Every claim the company examines must answer both: what is the net energy source — and where is the colder sink? A claim that cannot answer them is documented and set down. The mainstream prior on the underlying physics is “no”, and the program is designed to find that out cheaply and publish it either way.
Status marks used throughout
MarkMeaning
OriginalAn unedited original placed byte-for-byte, its SHA-256 recorded in the manifest and the source ledger. The file’s identity is verified; the statements inside it are not re-verified by placement.
VerifiedA specific statement supported by a located document whose identity (SHA-256) is recorded in the source ledger, or by a dated original communication read in full.
OpenA fact not yet established, or a decision that belongs to the founder, counsel, or a named person. Silence does not close it.
HistoricalAn earlier document retained unedited for provenance. Where it conflicts with a later document, the later one governs and the conflict is named.
DraftWritten for this package from the sources named at the foot of the page; not yet reviewed by counsel or approved for circulation.
[TBD] — …The founder’s marker — “make the TBD in brackets [TBD] and in orange ink” (15 Oct 2026) — for a current amount, term, role or detail not yet determined; the bracketed [TBD] is always printed in orange ink. It identifies an unresolved field; it does not establish a term, result, agreement or submission clearance, and no further question is asked of the founder about it. Written into the document where the value belongs and listed once in the register (Open Items Register, root; NEEDS_INPUT.md). Never a blank or a guess.
[Pending: …]Evidence, consent or an action owed by a named party other than a founder decision — counsel’s docket or answers, a person’s written consent, the xTech attachment check. “On hold” inside the marker records an express instruction not to act.
