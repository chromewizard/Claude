Why a bridge and not a seed

An investor who reads “not a seed round” and then finds a Seed Round folder beside this one should see discipline, not contradiction: the bridge is small because it is a bridge. The Seed Round is the round the bridge is designed to reach, and it is defined only once the entity exists, the first module is reviewed and the criteria are written. Its amount and terms are the founder’s decision and are marked open until taken.
