3 · Patent position — counsel’s advice of 5 October 2026, summarized
3 · What patent counsel said — verbatim, dated

The 2025 provisional expired on 27 February 2026 without a non-provisional filing, so it cannot support a priority claim. Emailing concepts to inventors and others is not a public disclosure; website material that has been public for more than a year cannot form the basis of an application. At concept stage little has been conceded, because patents protect how an invention operates, not concepts. The path forward: an invention record; a coversheet provisional application (approximately $2,000) filed in the inventors’ names and assigned to the company on formation; a non-provisional application (typically $10,000–$20,000) as the work matures. Counsel has filed no application to date; a domain and know-how assignment agreement follows formation.

#Dennis Majewski, K&L Gates, 5 October 2026
1“As you noted, the provisional expired after February 27, 2026. Since a non-provisional was not filed within the 1 year pendency period, it will not be possible to rely on the provisional filing for a priority claim.”
2“The emailing of the concepts to inventors and others is not a publish disclosure. However, the website is considered a public disclosure. Any material that has been publicly available for more than a year cannot form the basis for a patent application.”
3“Since the project is at the concept stage, it is very likely that you have not conceded much from the patent side. It is not possible to patent concepts. Instead, patents focus on how an invention operates or theoretically operates. This involves fairly detailed descriptions of the mode of operation and the key components involved in carrying out the operations. It doesn’t appear any of this has been developed or disclosed.”
4“Attached is a sample invention record form. Regarding pricing, we can file a coversheet provisional application for $2,000, which covers some app and claim prep. The cost to prepare a non-provisional application depends on the number of embodiments and length of the application. Typical ranges are between $10k to $20k.”
5“We would file the application in the name of the inventors, then later assign to the company after it is formed.”

On 29 September 2026 the same counsel confirmed the firm had filed no patent applications and had received no technical information for a filing, and that a domain and know-how assignment agreement can be prepared after EnergyWave is formed. No current Patent Center docket has been obtained. No later fee-authorization or filing evidence is included in this package; current authorization and filing status are not established. On 14 Oct 2026 the founder stated that “no patents have been filed” (S-38). That informal statement does not alter the documented provisional filing of 27 Feb 2025; its intended scope, and any later or current filing status, remain unconfirmed in this package and are counsel’s to establish from the docket.
