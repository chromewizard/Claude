1“As you noted, the provisional expired after February 27, 2026. Since a non-provisional was not filed within the 1 year pendency period, it will not be possible to rely on the provisional filing for a priority claim.”
2“The emailing of the concepts to inventors and others is not a publish disclosure. However, the website is considered a public disclosure. Any material that has been publicly available for more than a year cannot form the basis for a patent application.”
3“Since the project is at the concept stage, it is very likely that you have not conceded much from the patent side. It is not possible to patent concepts. Instead, patents focus on how an invention operates or theoretically operates. This involves fairly detailed descriptions of the mode of operation and the key components involved in carrying out the operations. It doesn’t appear any of this has been developed or disclosed.”
4“Attached is a sample invention record form. Regarding pricing, we can file a coversheet provisional application for $2,000, which covers some app and claim prep. The cost to prepare a non-provisional application depends on the number of embodiments and length of the application. Typical ranges are between $10k to $20k.”
5“We would file the application in the name of the inventors, then later assign to the company after it is formed.”

On 29 September 2026 the same counsel confirmed the firm had filed no patent applications and had received no technical information for a filing, and that a domain and know-how assignment agreement can be prepared after EnergyWave is formed.
