Characterize first. Condition second. Claim never, until the accounting closes. The plan for the first named research module.

The Oct-13 deck names Floyd Sweet’s solid-state design — the vacuum triode amplifier — as the first research module, and on 15 October 2026 the founder confirmed it as the lead research path (D2, ledger S-44): to be characterized, tested against the energy-conservation filter and then extracted or retired. This plan turns that slide into an experiment that can be pre-registered. It follows the baseline protocol in the Physical Architecture & Baseline Model v1.3 and the artifact-exclusion discipline of the September prospectus. It contains no performance claim and no device. Every input only the inventor can supply is marked [TBD] in orange ink — the founder’s marker for a detail not yet determined; no further question is asked.

1 · What is being tested, stated so it can be falsified
First testable question. Does a defined conditioning procedure produce a repeatable change in magnetic response or in loaded conversion performance of a core-and-coil assembly, compared with a matched control specimen that received a sham treatment? A measured change is engineering. A claim of additional energy requires full closure of the energy balance: Eall inputs = Eload + Eother outputs + ΔUdevice.

Three physical hypotheses are carried into the plan from the baseline model, each with the observation that would support or retire it:

HypothesisPhysical statementDiscriminating observation
H-A1 · Finite stored-energy depletionConditioning leaves the core in a non-equilibrium domain, exchange-bias or strain state; excitation triggers relaxation that releases finite stored energy.Output declines with cumulative delivered energy; remanence and coercivity change in step; the integrated surplus is bounded by a measured change in core state. Hysteresis loss is integrated over the core volume, not scaled from a single B–H loop.
H-A2 · Open-system transductionThe assembly harvests ambient energy — thermal gradient, RF flux or mechanical vibration.Output tracks the ambient term when it is varied or shielded: ΔT sweeps and calorimetry; characterized RF enclosure on and off; vibration isolation on and off.
H-A3 · Metrology phase skewProbe timing deskew in a high-Q circuit distorts the computed real power; an apparent surplus is an instrument artifact.Reading changes with frequency as the deskew model predicts; disappears when the whole measurement chain is calibrated and when power is computed from ∫v(t)i(t)dt rather than VRMS·IRMS·cos θ.

3 · The four-step protocol
StepWhat is doneRule
1 · One specimenSelect one concrete hardware specimen and record material, geometry, magnetic orientation and initial state. Photograph, weigh, measure. Prepare matched control specimens from the same lot.No measurement before the specimen record is frozen.
2 · Characterize before conditioningWinding DC and AC resistance; impedance versus frequency; coil coupling; temperature-dependent magnetic response; remanence and coercivity proxies on the control pair.Characterization drives can alter the core’s state: record every drive level; monitor initial and final states.
3 · Externally driven, loadedExcite within the characterized range; deliver power to the fixed resistive load; integrate input and output simultaneously: Eexc = ∫vexciexcdt, Eload = ∫iload²R dt. Map efficiency η = Pload/Pinput across frequency and drive.DC input is ∫v·i, never average V × average I. The deskew check (§4) runs at every frequency used.
4 · Conditioning as a separate experimentOnly after the unconditioned map is complete. Define the intended state change; meter total conditioning energy Econd = ∫vcondiconddt; apply a sham treatment to the control; repeat Steps 2–3 on both, blinded.Pre-registered before the first conditioning run; the comparison is conditioned versus sham, not before versus after.
4 · Energy accounting and the metrology check
Eall inputs = Eload + Eheat and other outputs + ΔUdevice, where U includes energy stored in capacitors, batteries, magnetic and strain states and internal thermal reservoirs. For steady operation (ΔU = 0) the efficiency is η = Pload/Ptotal external input; internal losses are already inside the input and are not added to the denominator. Closed-loop feedback with forward efficiency ηfwd < 1 and feedback fraction β ≤ 1 has loop gain T = β·ηfwd < 1 and decays geometrically; self-sustained operation without an independent input or stored-energy depletion does not occur.

Deskew benchmark (v1.3 §3, hypothetical): 10 VA apparent power, +50 mW true dissipation, 2 ns probe timing error — the computed real power reads +49.87 mW at 1 kHz, +37.43 mW at 100 kHz, 0 at ≈398 kHz and −75.66 mW at 1 MHz: a false “generation” reading. The plan therefore calibrates the complete measurement chain, computes power from sampled v(t)i(t), and repeats the loaded map with the probe pair swapped.

6 · Pre-registered decision rule
OutcomeDefinitionConsequence
PassA repeatable, conditioning-dependent change in loaded conversion or magnetic response, at ≥5σ against the empirical distribution of sham-treated controls, in blinded runs, surviving every catalogue row, in the full pre-registered population — and a closed energy balance that states exactly where any surplus came from.Report and data published; the module proceeds to Gate 2 conservation testing (closed-system calorimetry, second laboratory). No performance figure is stated.
KillNo change above the floor; or a change fully explained by one or more catalogue rows; or a surplus accounted for by stored energy or an ambient input.Published as a null or as a transducer result; the module is retired publicly, as the deck promises.
AmbiguousA change above the floor that does not survive one row cleanly, or does not reach 5σ in the full population.Reported as such; one bounded, pre-registered extension; a second ambiguous outcome is a kill.

  1. Specimen record and control-pair record (Step 1), signed by the inventor and the measurement lead.

  5. Conditioned-versus-sham report under the decision rule above, with raw data timestamped and hashed at acquisition.
