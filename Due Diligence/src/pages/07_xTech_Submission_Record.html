3 · What the paper proposes

An independent measurement platform to evaluate nanoscale energy-conversion claims before they drive Army power-system investment. First candidate: a published metal–insulator–metal diode and Casimir-cavity experiment (Moddel et al. 2021, 1.4 pW maximum, attributed to its authors; Maclay 2024 critique), tested beside matched controls under pre-declared criteria with guarded electrical measurement, artifact tests and thermal bounds. A concept-stage evaluation capability, not a power-source offering; a null result is an expected, useful outcome. Independent laboratory execution and a qualified technical lead are planned and not secured. A possible six-month feasibility sequence is described, gated on people, access and quotes.
