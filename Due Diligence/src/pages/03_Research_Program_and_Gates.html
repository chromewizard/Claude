Research first, claims last. One protocol, three gates, every thread under it.

The research question, precisely: do engineered structures — a conditioned solid-state magnetic core, a Casimir cavity, a metal–insulator–metal junction — produce any repeatable, artifact-free electrical output whose energy accounting cannot be closed by known sources? Expected answer, on the mainstream prior: no. EnergyWave exists to obtain that answer to a standard that survives independent review, and to publish it either way.

The gate protocol

Gate names follow the August 2026 white paper (Characterize / Conservation-test / Extract or retire); the September program documents number them Gate 1–3; the October deck shows them as Phases 0–3 with Phase 0 = this round. They are the same three decisions.

Energy accounting — the standard every thread is held to
Eall inputs = Eload + Eheat and other outputs + ΔUdevice — where U includes everything stored in capacitors, batteries, magnetic and strain states and internal thermal reservoirs. DC input is ∫v(t)i(t)dt, never average V × average I. Timing deskew between voltage and current probes in a high-Q circuit can turn a true +50 mW dissipation into a false −75 mW “generation” reading at 1 MHz; calibration must cover the whole measurement chain. A null result bounds the tested configuration at its demonstrated sensitivity — it does not identify an origin. (Physical Architecture & Baseline Model v1.3, §§1–3; Claude review v2, §3.)

Eight commitments, written in from day one
  • Pre-registration of every protocol, public and timestamped.
  • Null results published, with data, every time.
  • Replication budgeted and contracted before each gate, never assumed.
  • External scientific advisory board with veto over any performance statement — at least one declared critic.
  • No performance claim before Gate-3 third-party validation.
  • Capital released by gate (escrowed tranches on the next round).
  • Clean entity separation: EnergyWave, Inc. / McLean Companies LLC / GovThink.
  • Open methods: bench design and error-budget methodology published.
Why physicists expect “no” — said here before an investor’s physicist friend says it

The vacuum is the ground state. Casimir energy is a difference of zero-point energies and the force is conservative, so whatever work it does closing the plates must be paid back to open them. A device delivering steady work from a single equilibrium reservoir is a perpetual-motion machine of the second kind (Brillouin 1950). Dynamical-Casimir photons are real, and the pump pays for them. Even the best-case single-use Casimir stack holds about 2 J/kg against ~9×10⁵ J/kg in the lithium-ion cell a humanoid already carries. The program is designed so that if the prior is right, that is learned at Gate 1 for the price of the first tranche — and published.
