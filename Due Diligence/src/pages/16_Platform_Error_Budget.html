The platform is the asset whatever the gates find. Its error budget is a table of measured upper bounds.

The September prospectus commits the company to publish the measurement platform’s design and its error budget as a methods paper before any result is claimed. The budget assigns to every known way of manufacturing a picowatt-level signal a measured upper bound on the spurious output it could produce in the platform as built. The artifact floor is the conservative sum of those bounds, with a separately reported combined standard uncertainty for the statistical part; the platform is not commissioned until the floor is at least a factor of ten below the effect it is built to test. This page is that table with the method for each row and the bounds left to be measured.

1 · The commissioning criterion
Measurand. Electrical output power delivered to a defined load under stated operating conditions (bias, temperature, shielding, load impedance). Every row of §2 is expressed in that measurand: a bound on a current or a voltage is converted to power through the load model before it enters the budget. Artifact floor = Σ |boundi| over the numerical rows of §2 — the conservative arithmetic sum of absolute bounds, because systematic artifacts can add with the same sign; root-sum-square is used only for components shown to be independent, and the combined standard uncertainty uc (standard uncertainties with stated distributions, coverage and covariance, per the NIST guidance) is reported beside the floor, never in place of it. Procedural rows are controls and are excluded from the arithmetic. Each bound is measured on dead devices, twins and fixture-only runs in the platform as built. Commissioned when floor ≤ (reported effect) ÷ 10 — the September prospectus §6.3 rule, inherited and tightened here. For thread B the reported effect is 1.4 pW (Moddel et al. 2021 — inferred load power, one laboratory, not independently replicated in the reviewed evidence), so the plan proposes a floor at or below 0.14 pW — a commissioning target set by this plan, not an established requirement; the thread C lead and the advisory board ratify it at commissioning. 3 · Commissioning sequence
  1. Build and close the enclosure; measure shielding effectiveness versus frequency and the residual ambient field.
  2. Map the thermal field at the device site over 24 h; set the stability and residual-gradient targets in the pre-registration.
  3. Characterize the electrometer chain on open, short and known-null loads; record bias current and offset drift.
  4. Run dead devices, fixture-only and each twin class through the full measurement sequence; fill every bound in §2 from those runs.
  5. Compute the floor; compare with the criterion; publish the methods paper with the table; only then measure a live device.
4 · Calorimetry — commissioned in Gate 1, decisive in Gate 2

A differential calorimeter with a reference cell alongside the device cell, so that heat flow into the device can be measured while output is drawn. Its resolution sets what Gate 2 can say: if output is too small to resolve calorimetrically, Gate 2 requires the device count and integration time needed to resolve it before any conclusion is drawn.
