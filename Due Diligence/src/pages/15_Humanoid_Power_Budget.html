The load, computed, not asserted. Three-month objective #2, as a worksheet.

The deck’s claim is specific: a humanoid that runs human-grade sight and sound, smell and taste, touch and motion at once draws more than a lithium battery delivers. This specification turns that claim into numbers that can be checked. It defines the method, lists the published benchmarks with their sources, and leaves every subsystem load as an input to be measured or taken from a datasheet. No figure here is an EnergyWave measurement.

1 · Method
QuantityDefinition
Average electrical load P̄Σ over subsystems of (average power while active × duty cycle), plus housekeeping (compute, communications, thermal management, conversion losses).
Peak load PpeakThe coincident maximum — actuators under load with sensing and compute active. Sets the supply’s power ceiling and ramp rate.
Energy per shift EP̄ × shift length (default 8 h). The number that sizes a pack or a source.
Carried-mass equivalent mE ÷ pack specific energy (Wh/kg). Compared with what platforms carry today and with the biological benchmark.
GapThe ratio of E to the energy a platform-sized pack holds at today’s pack specific energy; and the runtime a platform-sized pack would give at P̄.

6 · What this specification is not

It does not say that any EnergyWave device meets any row of §5, and it does not describe one. It states the load. Whether a non-chemical source can meet it is the research question the gates exist to answer, and the fourteen orders of magnitude between the only peer-reviewed unexplained output in the literature (1.4 pW, one laboratory, unreplicated) and the hundreds of watts in §4 are the honest measure of the distance.
