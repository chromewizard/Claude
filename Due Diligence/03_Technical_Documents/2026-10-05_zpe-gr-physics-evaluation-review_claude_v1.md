## 1. Agreed

- **The arithmetic holds** (rechecked):
  - 8πG/c⁴ = 2.077×10⁻⁴³ N⁻¹ and ℓP² = 2.61×10⁻⁷⁰ m².
  - At a 1 µm gap, ℓP²/a⁴ = 2.6×10⁻⁴⁶ m⁻², or 9.0×10⁻⁴⁷ m⁻² with the 8π and π²/720 factors kept. That is a radius of curvature of 6.2×10²² to 1.05×10²³ m, or 6.5 to 11 million light years.
  - Earth's surface tidal curvature (1.7×10⁻²³ m⁻²) is about 10²³ times larger.
- **No measurable metric effect** comes from laboratory Casimir cavities under 1915 GR. This matches the engineering notes, where the best-case Casimir stack holds about 2 J/kg, single use (source note §4).
- **The argument covers gravity only.** Grok's own conclusion is limited to "useful gravitational effects". It says nothing about whether a magnet-and-coil specimen delivers more electrical energy than it takes in. That stays the bench question, settled only by first-law closure (review v2 §3).
- **Deskew metrology and the staged unconditioned baseline:** agreed, as in briefing v1.3.

## 2. Corrections

1. **Pais / NAWCAD.**
   - HAUC is not a test program and not a laser program. It is the patent's own name for the craft, a hybrid aerospace-underwater craft (US 10,144,532).
   - The Navy work ran Oct 2016 to Sep 2019 at about $508,000. The finding as stated: "NAWCAD determined that the 'Pais Effect' could not be demonstrated." Pais answered that the tests never reached the charge he considered necessary.
   - Replace "Empirically refuted" with "Not demonstrated in Navy testing (NAWCAD, 2016–2019)". That wording is accurate and harder to dispute.
2. **Grok's radius line.** The Grok text says the 10²³ m radius is "larger than the observable universe by a fat factor". It is about 4,000 to 7,000 times *smaller*, since the observable universe's radius is about 4.4×10²⁶ m. The conclusion survives, but "mathematically and dimensionally correct" needs this exception noted.
3. **"Near the Planck scale."**
   - For ℓP²/a⁴ = 1 m⁻², the gap is a = (ℓP² × 1 m²)^¼ = 4.0×10⁻¹⁸ m, or 0.004 fm. Write the scaling that way, not as √ℓP.
   - That gap is sub-nuclear, but it is 2.5×10¹⁷ Planck lengths, not near the Planck scale. Classical GR does not fail there.
   - What fails is the plate model. Real conductors stop reflecting above their plasma frequency, so the ideal-conductor ħc/a⁴ law overestimates more and more below about 0.1 µm. At femtometre gaps there are no plates at all.
   - a ~ ℓP is the condition for a curvature radius comparable to the gap itself, which is a different target from 1 m⁻².
4. **Sarfatti row: the claim is real but needs its source.**
   - Neither his email nor the Grok thread proposes metamaterials. Grok mentions them once, in passing.
   - The claim is his own published proposal: slowing light in a metamaterial amplifies the Einstein coupling (viXra:2206.0074). His figure is about 10⁶⁰, not 10⁴⁰.
   - The verdict stands. The c in 8πG/c⁴ is the spacetime invariant. A medium's phase or group velocity changes how light crosses the medium, not the coupling.
   - Cite the paper if the row stays. Drop "Fröhlich condensates" unless it is sourced.
   - Independent critique of metamaterial-coupling proposals in general: Rodal, arXiv:2507.09724 (2025).
5. **Status and entity name.**
   - Change "Approved Technical Briefing" and "definitive Council technical stance" to "Draft for council review" until Aura and Claude sign off.
   - EnergyWave is not yet formed. Write "EnergyWave (formation pending)", not "EnergyWave, Inc."
6. **Demarcation: agreed in principle, with two limits.**
   - Filings should describe structure and measurable behavior, not unproven mechanisms. But how the invention is described and claimed is Ian's decision with his patent counsel, not a council rule. The provisional already on file uses quantum-vacuum and antigravity language, so a "zero references" rule cannot apply to it retroactively.
   - "Non-equilibrium spin dynamics" is a mechanism label the inventor record does not contain. Under review v2 §7, mechanism, flux orientation and conditioning are inventor-defined, and reviewers do not name them. Until Ian defines one, use "solid-state magnetic materials and electromagnetic circuits" without a mechanism label.
