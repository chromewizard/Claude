## 1. "Zero Point Energy – Anti Gravity – Exotic Propulsion" (.docx supplied by Ian)

- **What it is:** an essay of about 220 words. It argues that "asymmetrical regauging" of Maxwell's quaternions is the basis for zero-point energy and field interactions, and it closes with an IP disclaimer referring to "this post".
- **Likely the filed description:** an embedded comment authored "USPTO" is Patent Center's DOCX upload feedback ("A supported heading was not detected"). It therefore appears to be the description file uploaded for 63/763,998 on 27 Feb 2025. Confirm against the Patent Center document list.
- **Defines no device:** it has no magnet, magnetization, winding, AC-flux direction, conditioning step, drawing or equation. Codex's orientation item stays "requires inventor definition" unless Articles 3 and 5 turn up.
- **Physics:** a gauge transformation leaves E and B unchanged, and with them every force and energy flow. Regauging by itself cannot be an energy source. Physically raising a potential is not a gauge change, and it costs work.
- **For counsel (internal):** "this post" suggests the essay was posted publicly. The date and venue belong in the disclosure history.

## 2. Salvatore Pais: Navy patent filings, 2015–2018

**Filings:**

- Granted:
  - US 10,135,366, electromagnetic field generator (Nov 2018).
  - US 10,144,532, craft using an inertial mass reduction device (Dec 2018; expired Jan 2023).
  - US 10,322,827, high-frequency gravitational wave generator (June 2019).
- Published applications:
  - US 2019/0058105, room-temperature superconductor.
  - US 2019/0295733, plasma compression fusion (abandoned).

**How US 10,144,532 was granted:** it was initially rejected. It was granted after NAVAIR CTO James Sheehy wrote that it was operable and that China was developing similar technology.

**Navy testing:** NAWCAD tested the effect from Oct 2016 to Sept 2019 for $508,000. The outcome: "NAWCAD determined that the 'Pais Effect' could not be demonstrated, and stated that no further research was conducted." Pais said the tests never reached the 1 C charge he considered necessary. For scale, 1 C on a 5 cm sphere means a surface field of 3.6×10¹² V/m, about a million times the field at which air breaks down.

**Relevance to EnergyWave:**

- The patents define no ferrite conditioning and no flux orientation.
- A grant does not establish that a device works.
- Patents assigned to the Navy are prior art and Navy property, so they are background only. This is a counsel item.

## 3. Exodus Propulsion Technologies (exoduspropulsion.space; Charles Buhler)

**What the site claims:**

- "125 uN of continious thrust in any direction it is pointed" (sic).
- "6 mg of weight loss".
- An "Exodus Effect" described as "repeatable, predictable, published and well-understood".
- A USPTO patent issued after a security hold.
- Unnamed "3rd-party validations".

**What press coverage adds (NextBigFuture, Mar 2026):**

- The claimed force is 5–10 mN.
- Tests used scales, force plates, pendulums, a sealed plastic box, and "vacuum chamber runs at multiple pressure levels".
- There is no peer-reviewed paper; the article calls skipping peer review "a deliberate choice".
- No independent lab is named. The only outside confirmation is witness affidavits given to patent examiners.

**What their tests don't rule out:**

- Ion wind or residual-gas effects, since no hard-vacuum pressure is stated.
- Electrostatic forces on the scale, the box and the surroundings. A sealed plastic box does not screen electric fields.

**The test that would discriminate:** thrust that persists in hard vacuum, inside a grounded Faraday enclosure, on a torsion balance, with polarity reversal and a matched dummy. This is the same test the principles file names for Townsend Brown.

**The governing point:** it comes from the Puthoff–Little paper Ian supplied. A stationary distribution of charges, currents and fields "cannot generate a propulsive force without emitting some form of reaction mass or energy, or otherwise imparting momentum to another system."

**Relevance to EnergyWave:** this is propulsion, not power, and no part of the energy-density thesis. It is not citable in EnergyWave material until it is independently replicated.
